Across eight datasets, each gate does a different job

Share of retraining's effect recovered by closing one gate alone (mean of 3 seeds; 1.0 means the whole effect). The structural gate carries direct neighbours; the contribution gate carries the forgotten nodes and the other clients. Closing both recovers all of it, exactly.

How this was made: closed-form federated model with PAGE's client counts (Louvain clients, seed 100), settings chosen on validation data. The request is the one used in the channel experiment: 10% of one client's training nodes, true labels, removed with all their edges. Readouts use every node of each region; the graph draws all forgotten nodes, their 1-hop neighbours, and a fixed random sample of 2-hop neighbours when there are too many to draw.

The 2-D map is a principal-component projection of the original and retrained models' outputs for the drawn nodes. Positions are exact at ×1; larger magnifications scale each node's offset from its retrained position. Forgotten nodes are always queried on the original graph, as an auditor would.
