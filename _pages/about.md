I am a Marie Skłodowska-Curie doctoral candidate at the [University of Naples Federico II](https://www.unina.it/) (UNINA), Italy, in the MSCA Doctoral Network [TUAI (Towards an Understanding of Artificial Intelligence)](https://tuai.eu/). My doctoral project, [DC9: Decentralised Graph Neural Networks: Adaptation, Training and Interpretability in Federated Environments](https://tuai.eu/doctoral-candidates/dc9), is supervised by Prof. Salvatore Cuomo (UNINA), with co-supervisors Prof. David Camacho (UPM), Prof. Shen Yin (NTNU) and Prof. Dariusz Mrozek (SUT).

**Research interests:** machine unlearning, federated learning and graph neural networks, with a broader interest in trustworthy AI, distributed systems and reinforcement learning.

Before my PhD, I was a Research Associate at the Gwangju Institute of Science and Technology (GIST), South Korea, working on machine learning for wireless power transfer and electricity price forecasting, and a Researcher at HT Core Co., Ltd., developing an XR-based training platform for clinical practice. I received my M.E. in Computer Engineering from [Chosun University](https://eng.chosun.ac.kr/eng/index.do), South Korea, in 2023, where I worked on reinforcement learning-based computation offloading for UAV-aided edge computing under the supervision of Prof. [Sangman Moh](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=6kVQbNIAAAAJ&hl=en). I received my B.Sc. in Computer Science from East West University, Bangladesh.
