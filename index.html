S M Asiful Huda

MSCA Doctoral Network TUAI, DC9

University of Naples Federico II

Naples, Italy

I am a Marie Skłodowska-Curie doctoral candidate at the University of Naples Federico II (UNINA), Italy, in the MSCA Doctoral Network TUAI (Towards an Understanding of Artificial Intelligence). My doctoral project, DC9: Decentralised Graph Neural Networks: Adaptation, Training and Interpretability in Federated Environments, is supervised by Prof. Salvatore Cuomo (UNINA), with co-supervisors Prof. David Camacho (UPM), Prof. Shen Yin (NTNU) and Prof. Dariusz Mrozek (SUT).

Research interests: machine unlearning, federated learning and graph neural networks, with a broader interest in trustworthy AI, distributed systems and reinforcement learning.

Before my PhD, I was a Research Associate at the Gwangju Institute of Science and Technology (GIST), South Korea, working on machine learning for wireless power transfer and electricity price forecasting, and a Researcher at HT Core Co., Ltd., developing an XR-based training platform for clinical practice. I received my M.E. in Computer Engineering from Chosun University, South Korea, in 2023, where I worked on reinforcement learning-based computation offloading for UAV-aided edge computing under the supervision of Prof. Sangman Moh. I received my B.Sc. in Computer Science from East West University, Bangladesh.
